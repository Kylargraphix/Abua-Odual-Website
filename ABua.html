Chairman's Welcome

I am honored to welcome you to the official website of Abua Odual Local Government, the largest and most dynamic local government in Rivers State. As the proud Chairman, I extend my warmest greetings to residents, investors, and visitors alike.

Abua Odual is not just a local government; it is a thriving hub of diversity and opportunity. Home to the prestigious zone, our community stands at the forefront of industrialization, hosting giants like the Dangote Refinery. We take pride in being the engine that drives the economic growth of Rivers State.

Beyond industry, Abua Odual is a beacon for real estate investors seeking promising opportunities. Our landscape is dotted with developments that promise not just financial returns but a chance to be part of a community that is writing the future.

For those seeking relaxation, our shores boast numerous beaches and resort centers, offering a perfect blend of tranquility and leisure. Abua Odual is not just where business thrives; it’s where you can find solace and rejuvenation.

On this, our official website, you will be able to explore our ongoing projects, learn about our community initiatives, and stay updated on the latest developments. We are committed to transparency, community engagement, and the overall well-being of our residents.

As we navigate the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead, I invite you to join us on this exciting journey. Whether you are a resident, investor, or visitor, Abua Odual welcomes you with open arms.

Thank you for being part of our story.

HON. ENGR. OWOLABI MICHAEL OFORI

OUR 4 CARDINAL AGENDA

Infrastructure • Local Economy • Social Development • Security & Environment

Our dedication is firmly rooted in the comprehensive advancement of Abua-Odual, guided by our 4 Cardinal Agenda: Infrastructural Development, Strengthening the Local Economy, Enhancing Social Services (Education, Health, and Social Interventions), and Promoting Security and Environmental Sustainability. Through these strategic pillars, we aim to cater to the varied necessities of our community, ensuring the well-being of our residents and fostering the collective progress of Abua-Odual..
